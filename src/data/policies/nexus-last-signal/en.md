# Privacy Policy · Nexus: Last Signal

_Last updated: November 23, 2026_

## Summary

Nexus: Last Signal does not collect, send to us or share any personal data. It has no ads, accounts, tracking or third-party tools.

## What stays on your device and in your iCloud

Game progress and settings are stored on your iPhone or iPad. If iCloud is on, a copy of your progress goes to the private iCloud database of your Apple Account so you can continue on another device. That database is yours: we cannot access it.

## Purchases

In-app purchases are made and processed by Apple. The game only checks which items you already own; we receive no payment data.

## Network and third parties

The game works offline. The only connections are Apple's own services (iCloud, the App Store and, if you sign in, Game Center). We use no analytics, advertising, tracking, social login or third-party crash reporting.

## Children

The game collects no data from anyone, including children.

## How to delete the data

Delete the app to remove the local data. To delete the iCloud copy, go to Settings > [your name] > iCloud > Manage Storage > Nexus.
